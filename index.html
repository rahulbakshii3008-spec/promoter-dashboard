Refer & Earn — Terms

  1. Commission: Aapko ek one-time commission milegi har us gym owner ke liye jo aapke referral link/code se signup kare aur GymCare India ka paid plan verify ho jaye. Commission amount gym ke chune gaye plan par depend karta hai.
  2. Lock-in period: Commission verify hone ke turant baad "Locked" status mein rahegi, aur 31 din baad automatically unlock hogi.
  3. Reversal: Agar referred gym owner apna plan cancel/refund kara le, to GymCare us commission ko reverse/cancel karne ka haq rakhta hai.
  4. Fraud prevention: Fake signups, self-referral, ya kisi bhi tarah ki dhoka-dhadi milne par commission cancel ho sakti hai aur account block bhi ho sakta hai.
  5. Payout: Unlocked commission ko aap withdraw request kar sakte hain. Payment UPI/bank transfer se manually process hoga, aam taur par 7–14 working days mein.
  6. Tax: Apni earnings par lagne wala koi bhi tax aapko khud handle karna hoga.
  7. Fair use: Sirf genuine, consent-based referrals bhejein — spam ya bina permission ke bulk messaging allowed nahi hai.
  8. Changes: GymCare commission rates ya terms ko future mein update kar sakta hai; naye terms sirf naye referrals par apply honge.
  9. Voluntary program: Ye program koi employment nahi hai, aur kabhi bhi discontinue ho sakta hai — koi guaranteed minimum earning nahi hai.

Payout UPI ID
